About

DH

I build displays that make you feel heat, touch, and motion on the skin, and study how people perceive them.

My research sits in human–computer interaction, focused on thermal-tactile haptics and multisensory perception for XR. I work across the full stack of a study: designing the experiment, building the hardware and software, and running it with people.

Next, I’m bringing haptics into games and live events: thermal feedback in controllers and keyboard-and-mouse setups, and recreating the feel of concerts, conventions, and exhibitions.

Research interests

  • Thermal haptics
  • Tactile illusions
  • Multisensory perception
  • XR / AR
  • Haptics for games
  • Wearables & embedded systems
