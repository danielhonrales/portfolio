About

DH

Hey! I’m Daniel, a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Dallas, working with Dr. Jin Ryong Kim in the Multimodal Interaction Lab.

I study human-computer interaction, building haptic interfaces and exploring multisensory perception. My work centers on thermal-tactile feedback for immersive XR, and I’m especially excited about reimagining games and live events through touch and temperature.

Before my Ph.D., I earned my B.S. and M.S. in Computer Science at UTD and interned twice as a software development engineer at Amazon.

Now Moving to Virginia Tech 🦃. Stay tuned for what comes next!
